Playing in four steps

On a Switch that runs homebrew. Nothing to sign up for.

1

Download EmuSwitch

Get EmuSwitch.nro from the latest release.

2

Copy it to /switch/

On your SD card, next to your other homebrew.

3

Add your games

Each console has its folder, like /roms/gba/. 3DS games go in /switch/dekopon/roms/.

4

Play

Open EmuSwitch from the Homebrew Menu and pick a game. Updates install from Settings.

On 3.0.0-RC9 or older? Install the next version by hand once: those versions couldn't replace their own file while running.

Legal

EmuSwitch is a legal emulator, we do not distribute any illegal ROMS and we do not condone piracy, all ROMS used for testing are ROMS that we have used from our own games.

EmuSwitch is still in testing and doesn't fully work yet. Expect bugs, crashes and games that don't run properly.
