Emulation on the Switch isn't going dark.

EmuSwitch is a free, open-source emulator launcher for Switch homebrew. 3DS games run built in, and ten more consoles open from the same menu.

Your whole library, sorted by console.

SETUP

Ready in a few minutes.

You need a Switch that runs homebrew. There's nothing to sign up for.

  1. Download EmuSwitch.nroFrom the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Copy it to /switch/On your SD card, next to your other homebrew.
  3. Add your gamesEach console has its own folder. A PS2 BIOS from your own console goes in ps2/, and decrypted 3DS CIAs in roms/cia/ or cias/ install themselves.
  4. Open it from the Homebrew MenuUpdates install later from Settings, General.
sdmc:/
├── switch/
│   ├── EmuSwitch.nro        the app
│   └── dekopon/roms/        3DS games

FOR THE 3DS

Everything you'd want from a 3DS emulator.

  • Save states from the quick menu
  • Save backups in Checkpoint's format, to and from a real 3DS
  • Cheats, mods (LayeredFS) and texture packs
  • Real amiibo, or .bin amiibo files
  • 1080p docked and resolution upscaling
  • Frame generation with LSFG
  • Screen layouts on R3, with a custom layout editor
  • Touch with a pointer, and full gyro
  • Per-game settings and full button remapping
  • USB drives for your games
  • Updates itself from Settings
  • Delete a game from its + menu
